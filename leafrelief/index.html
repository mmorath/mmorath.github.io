    Leaf Relief

    A paperless archive for your Mac

    Coming soon to the Mac App Store.

    Leaf Relief turns paper into an archive you can search – no account, no cloud, entirely on your Mac. Scan paper or drop files into an inbox folder: the app recognises the text, files each document under a name you choose, suggests title, type, sender, date, amount and tags, and finds everything again by full text, natural language or meaning.

    In one minute

    • Scan or import. AirScan scanners on your network and USB scanners with a macOS driver scan straight into the app; any other scanner, downloads or photos go through the inbox folder.
    • Searchable PDFs. Pages are turned upright, the text is recognised and written into the PDF as a searchable layer.
    • Filed your way. Ordinary PDF files in your own folder, sorted into the subfolders and file names you set up; tags and types as Finder tags.
    • Suggestions on the device. Apple Intelligence suggests type, sender, title, date, amount, deadline, IBAN and tags – on your Mac. Nothing is applied until you click.
    • Found again. Full text, natural language (“invoices last year over $100”), search by meaning, Spotlight and Shortcuts.
    • Evidence. Every filing and every page change is logged and signed with a key in the Secure Enclave.
    • No account, no cloud, no tracking. The developer receives no data from the app.

    The problem

    Paper arrives every week – invoices, contracts, payslips, letters from the tax office – and most of it has to be kept for years.

    Paper piles

    Folders and boxes fill up. Finding last year's insurance letter means leafing through all of them.

    Scattered PDFs

    What already is digital lies in Downloads, on the desktop and in mail attachments, under names like scan0042.pdf.

    No search

    A scanned page is just a picture. Without a text layer, neither the Finder nor Spotlight can find a single word in it.

    Cloud services that read your documents

    Many archive services solve this by uploading your documents and analysing them on their servers – your bank statements, medical letters and contracts included.

    In short

    Pain
    Paper piles finding one letter means searching every folder
    Scattered PDFs files everywhere, under meaningless names
    No search scanned pages are pictures without text
    Manual filing every document needs a name, a folder and tags by hand
    Cloud services your most private documents end up on someone else's server

    How Leaf Relief solves each of these

    What Leaf Relief does

    Leaf Relief does the whole path from paper to a searchable archive in one Mac app – and keeps every step on your Mac.

    Scan or import

    • Scan with AirScan scanners on your network and USB scanners with a macOS driver – the ones macOS itself knows. Feeder before flatbed, double-sided when the feeder can, 300 dpi, colour mode Automatic: pages without noticeable colour are saved in greyscale.
    • Follow every step: Scan → Text → File → Index, with the page count. You can cancel at any time.
    • Import from the inbox folder: any other scanner, downloads or photos – drop them in and Leaf Relief files them. PDF, JPEG, PNG, HEIC and TIFF are accepted; PDFs that already contain text are taken over unchanged, images and image-only PDFs get a text layer.
    • Text recognition runs with Apple's Vision framework in German, English, French and Spanish. Pages are turned upright first; the invisible text layer sits exactly over the image, so selecting and copying in Preview hits the word.

    File it your way

    • Subfolders by year, year and month, type, or year and type – or none.
    • File names from date, type, a name suffix and the page count; a name that already exists gets -2, -3 …
    • Ordinary PDF files in your own folder. Tags and types are Finder tags, the fields travel with the file. The app's index is only an index – the PDF is the source.
    • Your own types with name, colour and symbol, next to the built-in ones.

    Suggestions on the device

    • Apple Intelligence reads the document and suggests type, sender, title, date, amount, deadline, file reference, IBAN, a summary and tags – on your Mac, in the language of the document. No text is sent to a cloud model.
    • Without Apple Intelligence, simple rules suggest what they can: dates, amounts, IBANs with checksum, customer, invoice and contract numbers, and legal forms such as GmbH, Ltd or Inc. The rules understand German and English documents.
    • You decide. Suggestions are stored, never applied by themselves. Recognised fields are highlighted in the preview; one click applies them.

    Find it again

    • Full text across the whole archive.
    • Natural language – “invoices last year over $100” becomes filters for type, sender, period and amount.
    • Search by meaning, for when you remember what a document was about but not its words. Natural-language search and search by meaning understand German and English.
    • Spotlight finds your documents too; a hit opens the document in the app.
    • Shortcuts and Siri: “Scan Document”, “Find Document”, “Open Last Scan”.
    • Files only in iCloud are not downloaded; they are indexed from their name, fields and tags.

    Evidence: a signed change log

    • Every filing, import and page change – and changes made outside the app, for example in Preview – is recorded as an entry of a hash chain, signed with a key in this Mac's Secure Enclave.
    • A seal on each document says what is known: Unchanged since scan, Edited (n×), chain valid, or Verification failed with the reason.
    • Export a verification report and the public key.
    • Time stamps (optional, off by default): switched on, only a checksum (SHA-256) of each entry goes to a time stamping service – never the document.

    Editing pages

    • Rotate, move and delete pages – with undo.
    • The original is kept. Every edit is built from the original, so deleted pages can come back.
    • Fix Orientation Automatically turns crooked pages upright with the same recognition used during scanning.

    Deadlines and sharing

    • Deadlines as reminders – created only when you click “Create Reminder”.
    • Share and send by email as a copy named after the title, without your private tags and types.
    • A menu bar icon shows scanning, progress and the inbox count.

    Privacy

    Leaf Relief has no backend, no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your documents stay in the folders you choose; they leave your Mac only when you share them yourself. Details in the privacy policy.

    Requirements

    • macOS 26 or newer on a Mac with Apple silicon.
    • For suggestions with Apple Intelligence: Apple Intelligence turned on. Without it, simple rules suggest.
    • A scanner is optional: AirScan on your network or USB with a macOS driver; any other scanner works through the inbox folder.
    • In English, German, French and Spanish. Simple rules and natural-language search understand German and English documents.
    • Leaf Relief writes standard PDFs with a searchable text layer – no PDF/A.
    • One-time purchase, no in-app purchases. Coming soon to the Mac App Store.
