# Frameworthy

Frameworthy is a Mac app that prepares recordings for sharing: it converts a
video, adds a title bar and a classification bar with your own font and logo,
recognizes the spoken text, translates subtitles, proposes chapters and splits the
result into parts below a size limit — **entirely on your Mac**.

- Requires macOS 26 or newer on a Mac with Apple silicon.
- Text correction and chapter titles use Apple Intelligence (on-device).
- Available on the Mac App Store.
