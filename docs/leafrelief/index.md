# Leaf Relief

Leaf Relief is a Mac app for a paperless archive: scan paper or drop files into an
inbox folder, and the app recognizes the text, files each document under a name you
choose, suggests title, type, sender, date, amount and tags, and finds everything again
by full text, natural language or meaning — **entirely on your Mac**.

- Requires macOS 26 or newer on a Mac with Apple silicon.
- Scans with AirScan scanners on your network and USB scanners with a macOS driver;
  any other scanner works through the inbox folder.
- Suggestions use Apple Intelligence on-device; without it, simple rules suggest.
- German and English documents and user interface.
- Available on the Mac App Store.
