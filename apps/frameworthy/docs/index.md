# Frameworthy

*Share-ready meeting videos — made on your Mac*

**Coming soon to the Mac App Store.**

Frameworthy turns **screen and meeting recordings** into videos you can share
right away: a title bar and a classification bar in the picture, subtitles in
several languages, chapters to jump to and the right file size. All the work
happens **on your Mac** — your videos, audio and text never leave the device.

## In one minute

- **Title and classification on screen.** A bar with the title at the top, a
  bar with the confidentiality level (e.g. INTERNAL USE ONLY) and, if you like,
  your logo at the bottom.
- **Subtitles from the spoken text.** Recognized in German, English, French,
  Spanish or Italian, corrected by Apple Intelligence, translated on request.
- **Who speaks when.** Speakers are told apart by their voices, each with their
  own subtitle color if you like.
- **Chapters to jump to.** Proposed at pauses in the speech, titled by Apple
  Intelligence, editable.
- **The right file size.** Never larger than the original; long videos split
  into parts below a size you choose.
- **Entirely on your Mac.** No account, no server, no ads, no analytics.

## The problem

A raw screen or meeting recording is rarely fit to pass on as it is.

### Too large to share

An hour of recording quickly runs to hundreds of megabytes — too much for an
email, a wiki page or an upload limit.

### No title, no classification

Once the file is out, nothing in the picture says what it is or how
confidential it is.

### No subtitles, no chapters

Viewers can't read along, can't follow it in another language and can't jump
to the part that matters to them.

### Cloud tools want your recordings

The usual services for transcripts and subtitles work by uploading your
recording — often an internal meeting — to someone else's server.

| Pain with raw recordings | |
| --- | --- |
| Too large to share | email, wiki and upload limits reject the file |
| No title or classification | the picture doesn't say what it is or who may see it |
| No subtitles | no reading along, no other language |
| No chapters | no way to jump to the part that matters |
| Cloud tools | the recording has to be uploaded to a third party |

## What Frameworthy does

Open an MP4 or MOV, follow the steps, add it to the queue — and get a video that
is ready to share.

### Title and classification bars

A bar with the **title** at the top, a bar with the **confidentiality level**
(e.g. INTERNAL USE ONLY) and, if you like, **your logo** at the bottom. Choose
font, colors and bar heights; the preview shows the result instantly. The title
is suggested from the file name.

### Spoken text and subtitles

The app recognizes what is said — in **German, English, French, Spanish or
Italian**. Apple Intelligence fixes spelling and punctuation without changing
the meaning. Translate the text into other languages and **burn the subtitles
into the picture**, add them as **switchable tracks**, or both. You also get
**SRT and WebVTT** files.

### Who speaks when

The app tells speakers apart **by their voices**. Rename them and give each one
its own subtitle color if you like.

### Chapters to jump to

Chapters are proposed at **pauses in the speech**, with titles written by Apple
Intelligence. Everything is editable; chapters go into the video and into a text
list for your description.

### The right file size

The result is **never larger than the original**. If needed, long videos are
split into parts below a size you choose — with the part number “2/4” on screen
and the matching chapters and subtitles in every part. File names without
special characters play everywhere.

### Fast and convenient

- Hardware encoding on your Mac's media engine
- Queue: prepare the next video while the last one renders
- Step-by-step assistant or an expert view with every setting
- Interface in English, German, French and Spanish
- Full keyboard and VoiceOver support

## Privacy

No account, no server, no ads, no analytics. Speech recognition, correction and
translation use Apple's on-device frameworks. Only the speaker model (about
22 MB) is downloaded once from huggingface.co on first use — without any of your
data. Details in the [privacy policy](privacy/index.md).

## Requirements

- A Mac with Apple silicon and **macOS 26** or later.
- Correction and chapter titles need **Apple Intelligence** turned on;
  everything else works without it.
